# Review thoughts

Read the full review and inspect the reviewed work when it is available. Account for every finding in the reviewer's order.

For each finding, give:

a concise summary of the finding;
a faithful summary of what the reviewer says, including the claimed impact and evidence;
your assessment, stating whether you agree, partly agree, or disagree and why;
a specific proposed fix, or a clear reason that no change is needed.
Separate verified facts from inference. Note duplicate findings or shared root causes without merging findings away.

Treat this as analysis only. Do not edit the reviewed work unless the user separately asks for changes.
